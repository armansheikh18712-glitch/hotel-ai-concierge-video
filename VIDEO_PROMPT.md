# Hotel AI Concierge Video Prompt

## 30-Second Vertical Promotional Video

### Full Detailed Prompt

Create a 30-second vertical promotional video for a hotel AI concierge service. 

**Scene 1:** Hotel front desk with staff visibly overwhelmed as multiple guests ask the same repetitive questions in quick succession: 'Is there a room available? What time is check-in? What's the WiFi password?' The receptionist looks stressed and exhausted. 

**Scene 2:** Close-up of a smartphone screen displaying an AI chatbot responding instantly at 2 AM with perfect answers in multiple languages, with simple text responses like 'Room available,' 'Check-in at 3 PM,' 'WiFi password: …' The bot is fast, friendly, and available 24/7. 

**Scene 3:** Happy guest smiling in a modern hotel room, leaving a glowing 5-star review and a growth arrow rising upward alongside business metrics. 

**End Text Overlay:** 'Generative AI concierge for hotels. 24/7. Any language.' 

**Audio:** Upbeat, modern background music. English captions. 

**Style:** Cinematic, realistic, warm hospitality lighting, high-quality mobile-first vertical video composition, smooth transitions, polished commercial style, 4K, visually engaging, professional branding.
